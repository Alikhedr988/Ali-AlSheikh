— I — The project

Brilliant pieces, carrying a message for peace.

HIJAZZ HARB brought together a group of versatile musicians, all carrying a message for peace through their music. The tracks fuse the improvisational language of Jazz with the spiritual cadence of Sufi tradition — projecting the burdens of war into brilliant solos and ensemble passages.

The album was performed live and recorded in Beirut, Lebanon — a city that has held many of the contradictions the music speaks to.
